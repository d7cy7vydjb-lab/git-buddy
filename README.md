# Halvin Labs

Create a premium research peptide e-commerce store for European market 

(UK, Germany, Spain, Italy) with the following specifications:

DESIGN STYLE:

- Clean, minimalist, research-focused aesthetic (like Prism Labs)

- Professional trust signals (COA, purity, lab-grade)

- Modern sans-serif typography

- Color scheme: Dark background with accent colors (white, gold/cream, navy blue)

- Mobile-responsive design

HOMEPAGE LAYOUT:

1. Hero Section:

   - Headline: "Premium Research Peptides for European Scientists"

   - Subheading: "99%+ HPLC-Verified | Certificate of Analysis on Every Batch"

   - Call-to-action button: "Shop Peptides"

   - Background: Clean, professional lab imagery

2. Trust Signals Bar:

   - 99%+ Purity | Third-Party Tested | Fast EU Shipping | Discreet Packaging

   - Displayed prominently below hero

3. Featured Products Section (6 products):

   - Display as cards with:

     * Product image

     * Product name

     * Category tag

     * Price in EUR

     * Purity badge (99%+)

     * "Add to Cart" button

   - Products should be: Tirzepatide, Retatrutide, BPC-157+TB-500, Semaglutide, 

     CJC-1295+Ipamorelin, GHK-CU

4. Category Browse Section:

   - 7 categories as cards:

     * Fitness & Bodybuilding (29 products)

     * Anti-Aging & Skincare (10 products)

     * Medical/Pharmaceutical (13 products)

     * Wellness & Supplements (8 products)

     * Cognitive & Brain Health (3 products)

     * Hair Growth (2 products)

     * Solutions & Solvents (5 products)

   - Each category card shows product count and "Browse" button

5. Shipping & Trust Section:

   - Free shipping threshold: €300+

   - Same-day dispatch available (orders before 12pm)

   - Discreet packaging

   - HPLC/Mass Spec verified

6. Testimonials/Reviews Section:

   - 4-5 researcher testimonials

   - Rating display (5 stars, X reviews)

7. FAQ Preview:

   - 3-4 most common questions

   - Link to full FAQ page

8. Newsletter Signup:

   - Simple form: Email input + Subscribe button

   - Offer: "Get 10% off your first order"

NAVIGATION:

- Header nav: Shop | Categories | About Us | FAQ | Blog | Contact

- Mobile hamburger menu

PRODUCT PAGES:

- High-quality product images

- Detailed specifications (dosage, purity, format)

- Certificate of Analysis download button

- Batch/lot number

- Related products carousel

- Reviews section

- Stock status indicator

SHOP/CATALOG PAGE:

- Filter by: Category, Price range, Purity level, Stock status

- Sort by: Popularity, Price (low-high, high-low), Newest

- Grid view option

- Product count: 70 products total

CURRENCY:

- Display prices in EUR

- Show conversion for GBP, USD as reference

CHECKOUT:

- Simple 3-step process: Cart → Shipping → Payment

- Shipping options: Standard (5-7 days), Express (2-3 days), Overnight

- Payment methods: Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal

- Order tracking available

FOOTER:

- Links: Privacy Policy | Terms | Shipping Policy | Returns | Contact

- Company info: "Research-grade peptides for European scientists"

- Newsletter signup

- Payment icons

- Trust badges

PAGES NEEDED:

- Professional and scientific

- Emphasize research and laboratory use

- Clear disclaimers on legal research-only use
